General Terms & Conditions

Last updated: May 2026 | Version: GTC-NZ-2026-V3

These General Terms and Conditions apply whenever FG Finance Limited (“FGF”) funds insurance premiums or professional fees for the Borrower under a Premium Funding Credit Agreement.

1. Definitions

1.1 The Borrower in this agreement is also referred to as "you" or "your". If there are two or more borrowers, they are jointly and severally liable. The singular expression “Borrower”, “you”, and “your” includes all Borrowers and fewer than all Borrowers as the context may require.

1.2 “Account balance” means the total debit amount of your account with the Lender (FGF), including principal, interest, and charges. “Business day” means any day which is not a Saturday, Sunday, or a national public holiday in New Zealand.

1.3 “Default” and “in default” means that you have done something you should not have done or failed to do something you should have done, and you must remedy that act or omission in accordance with this agreement.

1.4 “Default Charges” are those fees and administration costs set out in Clause 9 or our published Fee Schedule.

1.5 “Default Interest” is charged at the annual interest rate plus an additional 5% per annum, calculated daily on the overdue portion of the account balance.

1.6 “Financial default” means that your scheduled payment has been made late, reversed, dishonoured, or not made, or you have failed to repay your account balance when formally called upon.

1.7 “Future Advance” and “collateral” have the meanings expressed in Section 16 of the New Zealand Personal Property Securities Act 1999, notwithstanding that that Act does not apply.

1.8 “Insurer” means the insurance company issuing any relevant insurance Policy.

1.9 “Interest” includes ordinary interest and Default Interest.

1.10 “Intermediary” means any person arranging any insurance or loan under this agreement on behalf of the Borrower and includes an authorized insurance broker.

1.11 “Policy” or “Policies” means the collateral insurance policies owned or to be owned by the Borrower for which FGF lends all or part of the premium to the Borrower.

1.12 “Scheduled payment” means the monthly or periodic instalment amounts payable by you to FGF on the dates specified in the Payment Plan.

2. The FG Finance Account

2.1 We will open and maintain an insurance premium credit facility for you. This means we will lend you such money as you may need from time to time for premiums on insurance policies arranged through your broker or intermediaries.

2.2 We will not release any funding until we are satisfied as to the terms, validity, and suitability of the underlying insurance Policy.

2.3 You authorize and request us from time to time to pay the insurance premiums on any Policy in such manner as may be required by the Insurer or the Intermediary, until you notify us in writing that you wish to cancel the insurance or repayment plan.

2.4 FGF can make a call for immediate repayment in part or in full if the Insurer refuses to issue any Policy, or the Insurer cancels any Policy, or if you enter default. Until FGF makes such a call for repayment, you agree to make all scheduled payments on their respective due dates.

2.5 You must notify us or your Intermediary immediately of any change to your address, telephone number, email address, or bank account details.

2.6 You must maintain a bank account which provides direct debit and/or automatic payment facilities so as to enable the periodic repayments required under Clause 6 to be made seamlessly.

3. Interest

3.1 We will charge ordinary interest on your account balance on a daily basis at the fixed rate set out in your specific Premium Funding Payment Plan. This interest rate remains locked for the duration of the loan term, providing you with certain and predictable costs.

4. Statement of Account

4.1 If this agreement constitutes a consumer credit contract under New Zealand law, we will send you statements showing your account in accordance with Sections 18 to 21 of the Credit Contracts and Consumer Finance Act 2003, and at such other times as we may decide or if you request a statement. Your account balance from time to time will be deemed to be the figure in your statement in the absence of manifest error. You must ensure you are aware of your account balance. For privacy and security reasons, we will verify your identity before disclosing details, and may follow up via email to your registered address.

5. How your Payments are Applied

5.1 We may appropriate any payment made by you in any manner that we decide in our absolute discretion, notwithstanding any direction you may provide. We will generally apply payments first to cover outstanding Default Interest, Default Charges, and then to reduce the principal loan amount.

5.2 If you fail to make your payments or to make up any deficit when called upon, we may proceed to request the cancellation of all or any of your funded insurance policies, and apply the returned pro-rata premium from the Insurer to reduce your account balance.

6. Payments

6.1 You agree to pay us not less than the scheduled payment on or by the due date in each month. You agree to provide us with and maintain a direct debit authority or automatic payment authority. If any due date is not a business day, payment will be processed on the next business day. Any deposits or credits will not be credited until they are cleared and fully honoured by the paying bank.

6.2 If we accept any payment or bank any cheque made or forwarded by you in purported full settlement or in terms connoting accord and satisfaction, we will not be deemed by such acceptance to have accepted the terms unless we have, prior to receipt, explicitly agreed in writing to accept the amount in full settlement.

7. Borrower’s Warranties and Indemnities

7.1 The Borrower warrants to FGF that all information given in connection with this contract is complete, true, and not misleading, and that the Borrower has made full and adequate disclosures of all relevant facts to the Insurer, and that such insurance policies are in full force and effect.

7.2 You authorize the Intermediary, immediately upon signing of this Agreement, to advise the Insurer of the assignment and power of attorney referred to in Clause 11, and request that the Insurer acknowledge receipt in writing to FGF.

7.3 You promise to indemnify FGF against any loss, cost, liability, or expense incurred in connection with any default under this agreement, or as a consequence of anything we do at your request or on your behalf.

8. Default

8.1 If you are in default, we may immediately debit your account with Default Charges as listed in Clause 9 and suspend your facility, preventing any future advances.

8.2 We will charge Default Interest on your account balance if you are in financial default, calculated daily from the due date until the default is fully remedied.

8.3 If you fail to make a scheduled payment, your obligation to pay continues even if we have debited your account with default fees. You agree to maintain sufficient funds in your bank account to enable us to clear all default fees, outstanding instalments, and default interest.

8.4 We may call up the entire loan facility immediately if you are in default. If you fail to pay on demand, you must pay all existing and subsequent enforcement costs as contractual damages.

9. Default Charges

9.1 Dishonour Fee: A fee of $30.00 NZD may be charged to your account if any scheduled direct debit payment is reversed, returned, or dishonoured by your bank.

9.2 Cancellation Fee: A fee of $100.00 NZD may be charged to your account in the event of default resulting in the formal cancellation of the loan facility and funded insurance policies.

9.3 Administration Fee: A default administration fee of $60.00 NZD per hour may be charged if our staff must spend time administering your default, including making calls, drafting notices, or managing recoveries.

9.4 Enforcement Costs: In the case of legal action or cancellation, you agree to pay all court and disputes tribunal costs, collection agency commissions, and actual solicitor fees incurred by FGF in enforcing this agreement.

10. Privacy Act 2020

10.1 The Borrower authorizes FGF and its agents to collect, verify, exchange, and disclose personal and credit-related information (both now and in the future) about the Borrower in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and our Privacy Policy. This includes sourcing details from Insurers, credit reporting bureaus (to check credit histories or report defaults), banks, and relevant third parties, for the purposes of evaluating creditworthiness, administering the loan, and complying with New Zealand AML/CFT legislation.

11. Specific Authority and Power of Attorney

11.1 You authorize and request us to pay the premium directly to your broker or Insurer. You irrevocably appoint FGF and any director of FGF to be your attorney to do anything you agree to do under this agreement. Under this Power of Attorney, if you are in default, FGF may cancel the funded policies, claim any return premiums from the Insurer, settle claims, and give receipts for proceeds. This power remains in effect until your account balance is fully paid and discharged.

12. Law

12.1 This agreement, its construction, and the rights of the parties are governed strictly by the laws of New Zealand. The parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the New Zealand courts.
