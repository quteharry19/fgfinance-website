Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance policies can be funded?

Almost all commercial and high-value domestic policies can be funded, including Business Interruption, Liability, Fleet, and Construction insurance.

What are the typical loan terms?

Repayment terms range from 6 to 10 months, providing structured cash flow options to suit your business requirements.

Is the interest tax deductible?

Generally, yes. For businesses, the interest and charges associated with premium funding are usually fully tax-deductible. We recommend consulting your tax advisor.

How do I apply?

Simply ask your insurance broker at FG Insurance Services or your current provider to include an FG Finance monthly payment option with your renewal quote.
