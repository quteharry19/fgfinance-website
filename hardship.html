Financial Hardship Policy

Effective Date: October 2024 | Version: FHP-NZ-2024-V1

What is Financial Hardship?

Falling on hard times or experiencing unexpected financial difficulties can happen to anyone. Whether it's a short-term cash flow setback or a more ongoing challenge, we understand that payment difficulties arise in many ways.

You may experience temporary or long-term financial hardship due to factors such as:

  • Injury, chronic illness, or medical emergencies.
  • Unemployment, redundancy, or business closures.
  • Reduced income or significant seasonal cash flow variations.
  • Natural disasters, adverse weather events, or regional crises.
  • Relationship breakups, domestic violence, or family bereavement.

Our Voluntary Commitment

At **FG Finance Limited**, we are committed to working constructively with our clients to find supportive ways to manage outstanding loan accounts during periods of financial stress.

While insurance premium funding operations are distinct from general long-term consumer home loan mortgages, we strongly support the ethical standards of the **Premium Funding Code of Practice** and voluntarily review and assess all applications for financial hardship support on a case-by-case basis.

Applying for Hardship Support

We encourage you to contact us as soon as you anticipate repayment difficulties. You can apply for hardship assistance yourself, or delegate authority to a nominated third party (such as a financial mentor, accountant, lawyer, or family member) to represent you. We will work with your representative in the same supportive manner we would work with you.

To assist us in assessing your situation, we may ask you to provide basic details regarding:

  • The primary cause of your current financial difficulties.
  • Your current monthly income and primary household or business expenses.
  • What revised, sustainable instalment amount you can realistically afford to pay.

Hardship Support Options We Can Offer

We analyze every application on its individual merits. Depending on your specific situation, the variations we may offer to your credit contract include:

schedule

Contract Extensions

Extending the loan term and spreading outstanding arrears over remaining or additional instalments.

do_not_disturb_on

Fee Waivers

Exempting you from dishonour fees, cancellation penalties, or default interest charges.

pause_circle_outline

Repayment Pauses

Agreeing to temporarily suspend all repayments for a defined, short-term grace period.

Our Assessment Process

We will review your request promptly. If we require additional verification or documentation, we will request it within 5 business days of your application.

If your request is approved, we will provide you with a written variation agreement outlining the adjusted repayment schedule. If we are unable to provide hardship support, we will clearly outline the reasons for our decision in writing via your preferred communication method.
