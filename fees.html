Fee Schedule

Alongside the interest on your loan, a few specific charges may apply to cover the real costs of administering your credit. They're all listed below, all in New Zealand Dollars (NZD), and these are our standard amounts.

credit_card Credit & Debit Card Merchant Fees

If you choose to pay your instalments by credit or debit card, the rates below cover the card-processing charges our payment providers pass on to us:

Card Issuer / Method Surcharge Rate Billing Frequency
payments MasterCard 0.90% Per transaction processed
payments VISA 0.90% Per transaction processed
payments American Express (AMEX) 1.50% Per transaction processed

We keep these card surcharges in line with the Commerce Commission New Zealand guidance on reasonable (non-excessive) surcharging. You can read that guidance at comcom.govt.nz.

gavel Administrative & Penalty Fees

These charges only apply when a payment fails or an agreement has to be ended early after a default:

Dishonour Fee $30.00 NZD

Charged whenever a direct debit, card instalment or cheque is returned unpaid by your bank.

Cancellation Fee $100.00 NZD

Charged if a funding agreement is ended early after an event of default. Note: we waive this fee entirely where the loan is used mainly for personal, domestic or household purposes.

trending_up Default Interest Rate

If an instalment isn't paid by its due date, default interest builds up on the overdue amount, worked out day by day from the due date until the instalment is cleared. The rate that applies is set out in your credit offer documents.
