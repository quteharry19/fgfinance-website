Customer Care Standards

A Guide to Resolving Complaints

Effective Date: February 2026 | Version: CRG-NZ-2026-V1

At **FG Finance Limited**, we are dedicated to providing excellent customer service. If we fall short of your expectations, we want to know. We have a robust, completely free dispute resolution process in place to address any complaints regarding our products, staff, agents, or service providers.

1 Step 1: Contact Our Internal Dispute Resolution (IDR) Team

Your first point of contact should be our credit administration team. Most questions or issues can be resolved rapidly over the phone or by sending an email. If our team is unable to resolve your dispute to your satisfaction, you may request a formal investigation by our designated **Internal Dispute Resolution Officer**.

Written Complaints Address:

FG Finance Limited, 430 Ulster Street, Beerescourt, Hamilton 3200, New Zealand

Phone: 0800 344 700 (Toll-Free)

**Our Promise:** If we cannot resolve your complaint within 5 business days, we will launch a formal internal dispute investigation. We will keep you updated in writing and aim to provide a final response within **30 calendar days** of receiving your complaint. If we require more time, we will contact you explaining the reasons for the delay and clarify your escalation options.

2 Step 2: Escalate to the Financial Services Complaints Limited (FSCL) Scheme

If our Internal Dispute Resolution process fails to resolve your complaint to your satisfaction, or if we have taken longer than **30 days** to issue our final decision, you have the right to escalate your dispute to an independent, external disputes scheme.

FG Finance Limited is a registered member of **Financial Services Complaints Limited (FSCL) - A Financial Ombudsman Service**. FSCL provides a fair, independent, and completely free dispute resolution service to consumers.

3 Step 3: Escalating Privacy & Data Complaints to the OPC

If your complaint strictly involves the handling, collection, access, or disclosure of your personal or credit-related information, and you are not satisfied with our privacy officer's investigation, you can escalate the matter to the New Zealand **Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC)**.
