A Guide to Resolving Complaints

Effective Date: February 2026 | Version: CRG-NZ-2026-V1

At FG Finance Limited we work hard to get things right, but if we don't, we want to hear about it. Our complaints process is free to use and covers anything to do with our products, our people, our agents or the providers we work with.

1 Step 1: Talk to our internal dispute resolution (IDR) team

Start with our credit administration team — most things can be sorted quickly by phone or email. If they can't resolve it to your satisfaction, you can ask for a formal review by our Internal Dispute Resolution Officer.

Written Complaints Address:

What we promise: if we can't sort your complaint within 5 business days, we'll open a formal internal investigation, keep you posted in writing, and aim to give you a final answer within 30 calendar days of receiving it. If we need longer, we'll tell you why and explain how to take it further.

2 Step 2: Take it to Financial Services Complaints Limited (FSCL)

If our internal process doesn't resolve things to your satisfaction, or we've taken longer than 30 days to give a final decision, you're entitled to take your complaint to an independent external scheme.

FG Finance Limited is a member of Financial Services Complaints Limited (FSCL) — A Financial Ombudsman Service. FSCL resolves disputes independently and at no cost to you.

3 Step 3: Privacy and data complaints — the OPC

If your complaint is specifically about how we've collected, used, shared or given access to your personal or credit information — and you're not happy with our privacy officer's response — you can take it to the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC).
