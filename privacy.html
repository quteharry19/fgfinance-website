Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

At FG Finance Limited, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information about you when you use our services, including:

  • Contact information (e.g., name, address, email, phone number).
  • Financial information (e.g., bank account details for direct debits).
  • Information related to your insurance policies and premiums.
  • Verification information (e.g., identification for AML/CFT compliance).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for purposes including:

  • Providing and managing your premium funding facility.
  • Processing your payments and managing your account.
  • Communicating with you about your account and our services.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements (e.g., AML/CFT).
  • Improving our services and developing new products.

3. Disclosure of Information

We may disclose your personal information to:

  • Your insurance broker (Intermediary).
  • Insurance companies (Insurers).
  • Credit reporting agencies and financial institutions.
  • Service providers who assist us in our business operations.
  • Government and regulatory authorities as required by law.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, use, or disclosure. We use physical, technical, and administrative security measures to safeguard your data.

5. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request the correction of any incorrect information.
  • Make a complaint if you believe we have breached your privacy.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and collect anonymous usage data. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of our site.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by posting a new version on our website. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
