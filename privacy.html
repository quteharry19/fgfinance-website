Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 | Version: PRIV-NZ-2026-V2

At **FG Finance Limited** ("we", "us", "our"), we are committed to safeguarding your personal and credit-related information with the highest level of security. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and disclose your personal details in accordance with the New Zealand **Privacy Act 2020** and the **Credit Reporting Privacy Code 2020**.

If you have any questions relating to this policy, or would like a printed hard copy, please contact our designated Privacy Officer whose details are provided at the end of this document.

notification_important Information Privacy Principle 3A (IPP 3A) Compliance

Under the *Privacy Amendment Act 2025*, **Information Privacy Principle 3A (IPP 3A)** governs the collection of personal information from a source other than the individual concerned (indirect collection). This framework is effective starting **1 May 2026**.

Because FG Finance operates primarily as an insurance premium funder, we collect clients' personal information **indirectly via authorized insurance brokers** (intermediaries) using their secure broker portals (`crm.fgfinance.co.nz`).

In compliance with IPP 3A, when an insurance broker transmits your personal or commercial credit information to us, we take reasonable steps to ensure that you are notified of, or are otherwise aware of:

  • The fact that we have collected your personal information from your insurance broker.
  • The specific purpose of the collection (i.e. to assess, verify, and administer your premium funding loan facility).
  • The intended recipients or categories of recipients of the information (e.g. credit bureaus, underwriting insurers, or clearing banks).
  • Your rights of access to, and request for correction of, your personal details.
  • Whether the supply of information is voluntary or mandatory under New Zealand law, and the consequences of not providing the details (e.g. our inability to establish your credit facility).

**Broker Obligations:** We require our partner insurance brokers, when submitting applications on behalf of their clients, to guarantee that the client has been provided with our Privacy Collection Statement and is aware of this Privacy Policy.

1. Types of Information We Collect

We collect personal and credit-related details only if it is necessary for our operations. Depending on the product or service we provide, the information we collect may include:

  • **Identity & Contact Details:** Name, email address, physical address, phone numbers, birthdate, employment details, and driver’s license or other government identifiers (to comply with New Zealand AML/CFT legislation).
  • **Financial Information:** Bank account details for direct debits, credit card details, credit histories, income details, and statements of assets/liabilities (if required for credit assessments).
  • **Policy Information:** Details regarding the insurance products you have taken out with your insurance broker, including insurer names, policy durations, premium amounts, and policy numbers.
  • **Credit Reports:** Credit reporting data sourced from registered credit bureaus in New Zealand, which may include payment history records and credit evaluations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for authorized, business-related purposes, including:

  • To process, evaluate, and verify credit applications.
  • To establish, administer, and manage your premium funding facility (including processing repayments and automated direct debits).
  • To monitor credit worthiness and enforce debt collection processes where necessary.
  • To comply with our regulatory obligations under New Zealand law (such as the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Financing of Terrorism Act 2009).
  • To facilitate administrative operations, including system testing and consumer surveys.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We may disclose your personal details to external organizations, strictly under secure conditions and for relevant purposes, including to:

  • Your nominated **insurance broker** (intermediary) and the underlying **insurance companies** underwriting your policy.
  • Registered New Zealand **credit reporting bodies** (such as Equifax, illion, or Centrix) to perform credit checks or report defaults.
  • Secured mailing houses, IT service providers, administrative contractors, debt recovery agencies, and lawyers.
  • Government, regulatory, or law enforcement bodies in New Zealand where required or permitted by law.

4. Accessing and Correcting Your Information

Under the New Zealand **Privacy Act 2020**, you have the absolute right to request access to the personal and credit-related information we hold about you. You also have the right to request that we correct any incorrect or outdated details.

To ensure confidentiality, we will verify your identity thoroughly before releasing any data. While accessing your information is completely free of charge, we may recover reasonable administrative expenses incurred (such as photocopying or search time) if your request requires substantial administrative effort.
