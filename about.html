The Journey

Our Story of Growth

Founded in early 2025, our rapid evolution reflects a modern approach to financial services and global collaboration.

January 2025

Company Foundation

FG Finance Limited was incorporated in Hamilton, New Zealand, with a vision to streamline commercial premium funding for Kiwi businesses through modern technology and transparent pricing.

April 2025

Global Support Infrastructure

To deliver rapid turnaround times and 24/7 responsiveness, we established dedicated operations and back-office support hubs across Australia and India alongside our NZ headquarters.

August 2025

Digital Platform & Broker Portal

Launched our custom broker funding portal and automated quoting engine, enabling insurance brokers to generate instant funding contracts for client policy renewals.

November 2025

FG Group Ecosystem Integration

Full integration into the broader FG Group ecosystem alongside FG Insurance Services (FGIS) and FG Life, building a unified financial services suite for Kiwi businesses.

Early 2026

Fixed Rate & Fee Standardisation

Introduced transparent flat-rate pricing (6.75% across all tenures) with a low $20 application fee, setting a new benchmark for clarity and ease in insurance premium funding.
