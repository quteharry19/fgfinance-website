The Journey

Our Story of Growth

From a small local startup to a leading premium funding provider, our commitment to service has never wavered.

2008

Foundation

FG Finance Limited was established with a simple mission: to provide New Zealand businesses with a smarter way to manage their annual insurance premiums. We recognized that lump-sum payments often restricted business growth and set out to create a flexible alternative.

2014

Expansion & Technology

We introduced our specialized funding platform designed specifically for the New Zealand broker market. This allowed brokers at FG Insurance Services and other leading NZ firms to provide instant funding quotes, significantly speeding up the renewal process for clients.

2020

Weathering the Storm

During global uncertainties, we stood by our clients, offering flexible repayment terms and hardship support, proving we are partners first.

Today

A Complete Ecosystem

Today, FG Finance Limited is a key pillar of the FG Group. Working alongside FG Insurance Services (FGIS) and FG Life, we provide a comprehensive financial ecosystem that supports the long-term resilience and growth of businesses across Aotearoa.
