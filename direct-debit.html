Direct Debit Framework

Direct Debit Service Agreement

Effective Date: September 2024 | Version: DD-NZ-2024-V1

This Direct Debit Service Agreement outlines our commitments to you as your premium funding credit provider, and your corresponding obligations when establishing an automated direct debit authority with **FG Finance Limited**. It covers direct debits established on New Zealand bank accounts through authorized clearing banks (including ANZ, ASB, Westpac, BNZ, Kiwibank, and others).

1. Our Commitments to You

We will periodically withdraw repayments from your nominated bank account strictly in accordance with the payment schedule outlined in your **Offer to Borrow for Insurance Premiums** or subsequent approved loan adjustment schedules.

  • **Holiday Adjustments:** If a scheduled instalment falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or a public holiday in New Zealand (including regional anniversary days), the debit will be processed by your bank on the **next consecutive business day**.
  • **Notice of Alteration:** We will issue at least **14 days' written notice** to you prior to making any adjustments to the billing frequency, debit amounts, or direct debit terms of this agreement.
  • **Strict Confidentiality:** We will keep all your bank account, credit card, and transactional details secure and private, disclosing them strictly to satisfy bank clearing requirements, credit assessment checks, or as required by New Zealand law.

2. Your Obligations

By signing a Direct Debit Authority or submitting an online acceptance, you agree to fulfill the following terms:

  • **Account Eligibility:** Ensure your nominated account can accept direct debits. Some specialized savings accounts, investment products, or credit accounts may not support automated direct debits. Check with your bank first.
  • **Funds Availability:** You must guarantee that your account contains **sufficient cleared funds** by 5:00 PM on the business day prior to the scheduled payment date.
  • **Returned Payment Penalties:** If a payment is returned as failed or dishonoured by your bank due to insufficient funds, a dishonour charge of **$30.00 NZD** will be added to your outstanding loan balance, and default interest may begin to accrue.

3. Deferring or Modifying Payments

If you need to defer, alter, or cancel a scheduled direct debit repayment, you must notify FG Finance Limited in writing **at least 5 business days prior** to the payment date.

Please submit requests to admin@fgfinance.co.nz or connect with your designated insurance broker to submit a modification request through our broker portal.

4. Dispute Mechanism

If you identify a billing discrepancy or believe a direct debit has been initiated in error or processed on the wrong date, please connect immediately with our credit administrator. We will investigate the transaction and resolve the issue within **5 business days**.

If we identify that an error has occurred, we will immediately credit your bank account with the disputed amount (including any processing fees you incurred). If we find that no error occurred, we will provide you with a written explanation including bank transaction records.
